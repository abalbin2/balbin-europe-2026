Stop 01 — Arrival
Valencia
Spain
📅 May 8 – 10  ·  3 nights

Your trip begins in Valencia — Tony & Sarah's home. Spain's third-largest city sits on the Mediterranean coast and is famous for its futuristic architecture, incredible food scene (the original home of paella), and year-round sunshine. Take it easy, shake off the jet lag, and enjoy the city before the Italy adventure begins.

Florence
Tuscany, Italy
📅 May 15 – 17  ·  2 nights

Florence is the cradle of the Renaissance — a compact, walkable city overflowing with masterpieces. Michelangelo's David, Botticelli's Birth of Venus, the Uffizi, and some of the finest bistecca in Italy. The whole family is here together, staying at Tony's company's own property in the heart of the city.

Stop 04
Lucca
Tuscany, Italy
📅 May 17 – 19  ·  2 nights

One of Tuscany's best-kept secrets. Lucca is a small, perfectly preserved medieval city enclosed by Renaissance walls wide enough to walk or cycle on top of. Less crowded than Florence, deeply charming, and a fantastic base for a day trip to Pisa's famous Leaning Tower — just 30 minutes away by train.

Stop 05
Santa Margherita Ligure
Italian Riviera — Base for Cinque Terre & Portofino
📅 May 19 – 22  ·  3 nights

A beautiful, understated fishing town on the Ligurian coast — less crowded than its famous neighbours but perfectly placed for day trips to both Cinque Terre and Portofino. Three nights here means you can explore each one without rushing. The pastel-coloured harbour, fresh seafood, and warm Ligurian evenings make this a highlight of the trip.

Stop 06
Lake Como
Azzano / Mezzegra, Lombardy
📅 May 22 – 25  ·  3 nights

Lake Como is one of Italy's most beautiful places — dramatic Alps dropping straight into deep blue water, dotted with pastel villages and grand 19th-century villas. You're staying in the quiet hamlet of Azzano, above Lenno, at a private villa on the lake's quieter western shore. Villa Balbianello — one of the most filmed locations in the world — is a short walk away.
